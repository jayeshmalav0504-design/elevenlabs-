How to Clone Virat Kohli, Rohit Sharma & Ravi Shastri in ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs allows you to clone any distinct voice using their Instant Voice Cloning (VoiceLab) feature with just 1–2 minutes of clean audio!

1

Get 1–2 Minutes of Clean Speech Sample

Find a high-quality interview, post-match conference, or commentary clip without background music or stadium crowds:

  • Virat Kohli: Post-match press conferences (e.g. ICC or IPL pressers), RCB podcast interviews.
  • Rohit Sharma: Captain's press conferences (expresses his relaxed, humorous Mumbai tone).
  • Ravi Shastri: Commentary highlights or podcast episodes (pure baritone voice).
2

Upload to ElevenLabs VoiceLab

Go to elevenlabs.io/app/voice-lab and click "Add Generative or Cloned Voice" → Choose "Instant Voice Cloning".

Upload your audio file, name it (e.g. Virat Kohli Clone), and click Create Voice.

3

Copy the Voice ID & Paste Here

In ElevenLabs VoiceLab, click on the three dots (...) next to your newly created voice and click "Copy Voice ID".

Paste it into the "Custom Voice ID" field in CricVoice Studio, and it will immediately generate speech with your cloned voice!

4

Built-in Matching Fallbacks

Don't have custom cloned voice files yet? No problem! CricVoice Studio comes pre-configured with the closest matched high-fidelity neural voices and fine-tuned stability, clarity, and style exaggeration parameters to deliver authentic pacing for all three legends immediately!
